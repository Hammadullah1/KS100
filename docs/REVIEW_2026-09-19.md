## Two defects repaired

### 1. Signal thresholds could never qualify

The selection stage has at most 63 dates, reduced further when overlapping labels are purged. Its old minimum was 100 selected dates. Even perfect predictions could not meet it.

Protocol v2 separates exploratory policy fitting (20 selected observations across 20 dates) from independent evidence for a high-correctness claim. The latter still requires at least 200 selected observations across 100 dates, the declared 80% lower confidence bound, coverage, baseline improvement and calibration checks. A fitted threshold alone cannot enable a validated directional signal.

Regression tests prove that a qualifying small tuning sample can fit thresholds while still failing the final accuracy-claim gate. Frozen old configurations retain their original behaviour. No old results were rewritten.

### 2. Rolling company reports split by rows

The runner divided predictions into groups of 63 rows. With several companies, that is fewer than 63 dates and can split a date's observations across groups.

Reports now use complete chronological date windows and preserve the original row indices for matching probabilities. Tests cover shuffled two-company data, complete coverage, non-overlapping dates and invalid window sizes.
